# Report template

Use this structure for analytical answers. Shorten it for quick follow-up
questions.

1.  **Key insight:** One or two sentences that answer the question with the
    decisive number and its interval.
2.  **Evidence:** A compact table, for example:

    Promotion | Incremental sales (95% interval) | ROI on spend | Direction
    --------- | -------------------------------- | ------------ | ---------
    ...       | ...                              | ...          | ...

3.  **Recommended actions:** Up to three concrete actions. For each, give the
    segment or period, the expected direction of impact and the supporting
    number.
4.  **Confidence and caveats:** Robustness (stability and sensitivity to
    confounding), low-support results, missing margins, and
    confounders that were not adjusted for.
5.  **Next step:** The analysis or test that would most reduce the remaining
    uncertainty.
